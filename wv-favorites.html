Mountain State Pride

Did You Know?

🏛️

Capital Since 1885

Charleston became the permanent capital of West Virginia in 1885, cementing its role as the heart of Mountain State life.

🌊

The Kanawha River

The Kanawha River helped shape Charleston as a trade and cultural hub — its banks have witnessed generations of community history.

⛰️

Always Wild, Always Wonderful

West Virginia's motto — Montani Semper Liberi — means "Mountaineers Are Always Free." A quiet pride runs through everything here.

🎉

A Festival City

Charleston hosts some of the state's most beloved annual festivals — from Vandalia Gathering to FestivALL — celebrating local arts, music, and heritage.

🤝

Community First

Neighbors helping neighbors isn't a slogan here — it's how Charleston operates, generation after generation, morning after morning.

🍩

Part of the Story

For decades, Donut Connection has been one of Charleston's quiet morning rituals — a place where the same families return year after year.
